# Holstein_LongRead_SNP_SV_Analysis

## 1. Introduction
In 1964, a Holstein breeding experiment at the University of Minnesota established a contemporary selected line (Contemporary Holsteins, CH) and an unselected control line preserving the genetic composition of the 1960s (Unselected Holsteins, UH). UH cows produce less milk but exhibit superior overall health compared with CH cows. This study aimed to detect and characterize SNPs, short indels, and structural variants (SVs) from long-read ONT sequencing data in UH and CH cows, representing more than 50 years of divergence under selection in U.S. Holsteins. By comparing CH with the UH, we investigated the genomic impacts of long-term selection, identified selection signature regions across the Holstein genome.

## 2.Analysis pipeline
This repository contains the bioinformatics pipeline and analysis scripts for a genomic study of Contemporary Holstein (CH) and Unselected Holstein (UH) cattle using ONT long-read whole-genome sequencing. The project characterizes SNPs, indels, and SVs in each population and identifies genomic selection signatures associated with long-term selection.
